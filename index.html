Actionable Findings for Academic Administrators & Instructors

01

Internal Assessment as the Golden Indicator

Continuous internal marks account for over 36% of predictive weight. Midterm test results are the single earliest and most reliable proxy for final failure, highlighting the urgency of conducting early week-4 evaluations.

02

The 75% Attendance Cliff

Student attendance exhibits a pronounced non-linear threshold effect: when attendance falls below 75%, probability of being classified "At Risk" triples from 6.2% to 27.8%, requiring automated absence alerts.

03

Cognitive Rest & Study Equilibrium

Weekly study hours exhibit diminishing returns beyond 22 hours/week if combined with severe sleep deprivation (<5 hours/day). Intervention strategies must promote sustainable study hygiene rather than sheer cramming.
